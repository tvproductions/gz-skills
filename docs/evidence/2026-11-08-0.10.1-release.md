`f49c765831fecfbd058aaf7893206cda3ff1890a`. Remote inventory found no v0.10.1

Bundle mirrors and uv-generated lock agree at 0.10.1. No new capability,
stable promotion, runtime-family change or consumer migration is included.

and advances both marketplace pins and current installation examples together.
